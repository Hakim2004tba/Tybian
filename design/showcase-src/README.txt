Full screenshots of the running platform, as captured (Safari chrome,
Supabase tab strip and the signed-in avatar all still in frame).

They live HERE and not under public/ deliberately: anything under public/
is served to the world at its path, and these carry a real account's name
and browser state. Only the cropped derivatives in public/showcase are
published.

The crop boxes used for each are in the session that produced them; to
re-crop, open the original and cut a 16:10 rect around the control the
row describes, then overwrite public/showcase/<name>.png.
